Background

Lively is a mindfulness app designed to transform traditional in-person mental wellness experiences into a seamless digital format.

Originating from a Malaysian-based parent organization, the idea was to make practices like meditation, sound healing, and wellness retreats accessible to users anytime, anywhere.

Contribution

I took full ownership of Lively, leading the project from start to finish. I worked closely with stakeholders to align user needs with business goals.

I took full ownership of Lively, leading the project from start to finish. I worked closely with stakeholders to align user needs with business goals.

This involved conducting workshops and discovery calls to understand the market, users, and business vision. I managed project workflows, UX research, wireframing, and UI design, ensuring a seamless and successful product launch.

Results

Turning Mindfulness into a Digital Reality

Lively evolved into an integrated digital platform delivering tangible mindfulness experiences via the internet. The application's intuitive interface, supplemented by directed meditations, auditory therapy, and virtual getaways, resulted in elevated user contentment and established Lively as the most financially rewarding project for our entity.
