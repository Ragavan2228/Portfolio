Background

Consark is a finance platform offering solutions for financial close, risk reporting, and compliance, serving clients in tech,
ed-tech, and automotive sectors. While functional, the platform lacked a refined user experience. This redesign aimed to enhance usability, aesthetics, and scalability through structured UX research, prototyping, and a cohesive design system.

My Contribution

I led end-to-end UX and UI design, including discovery sessions, market research, stakeholder analysis, wireframing, and UI design. Additionally, I developed a scalable design system and collaborated closely with stakeholders and developers for a seamless handoff.

Problem

Consark’s platform faces usability challenges, with workflows, navigation, and broken user journeys making everyday tasks cumbersome. This leads to frustration, reduced productivity, and lower engagement among clients.

Consark’s platform faces usability challenges, with workflows, navigation, and broken user journeys making everyday tasks cumbersome. This leads to frustration, reduced productivity, and lower engagement among clients.

As competition grows, improving the platform’s efficiency and user experience is critical to retaining customers and driving business growth.

Idea

Wedding between Excel and Jira

Finance professionals rely on Excel for reports and audits, while Jira streamlines requests and workflows. Our approach fused these two worlds, crafting a seamless experience that balanced the flexibility of spreadsheets with the structure of workflow management—ensuring efficiency without disrupting familiar processes.

Results

A Smarter, Sleeker Consark

Through continuous collaboration with developers, stakeholders, and real users, we redefined Consark into a sleek, scalable, and intuitive platform. With improved navigation, enhanced workflows, and a polished experience, the product became more efficient and user-friendly. Despite multiple iterations, we delivered a refined solution within the set timeline, proving that great design is built through collaboration.
